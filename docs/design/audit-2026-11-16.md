# Design guide audit, 2026-11-16 (Mac, Chrome, read only)

Measured the live `habib-wh` and `habib-mkh` dashboards against `docs/design-guide.md` Draft v0.1 and `docs/design/habib-tokens.yaml`. Nothing was tapped, typed or changed. HA core 2026.11.1, address `https://ha.thehabibfam.cc`, signed in as the owner (admin), 21:28 to 21:35 CST.

## Method and limits

1. **Viewports were set with a same-origin frame, not by resizing the window.** The Mac screen is 1470 × 956 and Chrome was at 110% zoom, so the window could only reach 551 to 1336 CSS px wide. Each dashboard was loaded in a frame sized to the exact target (430 × 932 up to 2560 × 1440) and scaled down to fit the screen. Media queries and layout respond to the frame size, so all 12 target sizes were reached exactly.
2. **The HA sidebar was docked** (256 px) at 1024 px and wider, as it is for the owner on a laptop. It takes width from the view, so column counts are given both as measured and without the sidebar.
3. **Chrome on a Mac does not reproduce iOS Safari or the companion app**: no safe areas, no dynamic toolbar, no touch, a pixel ratio of 2.2 and not 3. The owner's iPhone and iPad checks stay required.
4. **Light-mode emulation was not possible** with the tools available (no DevTools rendering emulation). Part E reports what could be read instead.
5. Screenshots are 1336 × 678 at most (the frame scaled to the screen). Six sizes per dashboard were saved at that size, the rest smaller.
6. Contrast figures come from sampling JPEG screenshots; treat them as ±0.3.

### Findings from Part B

1. **Column rule (fits all 27 measurements):** columns = the smaller of 4 and floor((view width − 32) ÷ 352). So the view needs 736 px for 2 columns, 1088 px for 3 and 1440 px for 4. A section is at least 320 px and at most 500 px wide; column gap 32 px. Above 4 × 500 the block is centred with empty margins (2560: 360 px left margin after the sidebar).
2. **The sidebar changes the answer.** v0.1 says 1440 gives 4 columns; that is true only with the sidebar hidden. With the owner's docked sidebar 1440 and 1512 give 3, and 1728 is the first Mac size with 4.
3. **Wall tablet, 1920 × 1080 panel.** With the sidebar docked: 960 × 540 (2x) gives 1 column, 1280 × 720 (1.5x) gives 2, 1920 × 1080 (1x) gives 4. With the sidebar hidden (kiosk user or a collapsed sidebar), by the rule: 2, 3 and 4. Not measured without the sidebar, because hiding it means changing an HA setting.
4. **Blank dashboard in a 1 px band on habib-mkh.** At a frame width of 699 the real width was 699.09 px (110% zoom) and neither query matched: `(max-width: 699px)` and `(min-width: 700px)` leave every width between 699 and 700 uncovered. The view rendered wallpaper only. Any device with a fractional CSS width in that band (browser zoom, Windows or Android scaling) gets an empty dashboard. Source: `dashboards/habib-glass-build.py` lines 63 and 64.
5. **MKH on one phone screen holds.** At 430 × 932 the last card ends at 740 px (v0.1 said about 720; the 2 px taller Climate bar and the 3-chip rows at 50.8 px explain the difference). 192 px to spare in Chrome; the iPhone's toolbars will take part of that.
6. **Dock bar text wraps badly at 430.** In the compact dock the Climate bar reads "Down 71° Up" then "70°" on a second line, grows to 54 px against 52 for its neighbours, and its label sits 1 px from the top edge. The Listen bar wraps "Nothing playing" onto 2 lines. See `mkh-0430-dock-zoom.png`.
7. **Prayer fold bar says "Times unavailable" every evening.** All six prayer sensors held valid times for 2026-11-16. After Isha (18:42 CST) no prayer is left today, the card finds no "next" and prints "Times unavailable" until the sensors roll over (last change 00:16 CST). The times are available; the wording is wrong for about 5.5 hours a day. Source: `www/habib/habib-glass.js` line 485. habib-wh only (habib-mkh has no prayer card). The Adhan automations are not affected by this.
8. **Hero leaves a hole on wide habib-mkh.** With 2 or more columns the hero section is 262 px tall beside a 462 px Home section, so about 200 px of bare wallpaper sits under the clock (habib-wh fills it with the Prayer bar, 341 px against 274).
9. **Leftover resources still load on every page:** `nuvio.js` (and `nuvio-core`), Material You Utilities 2.2.0 and Material Symbols (six icon lists fetched). None is used by the glass cards.

### Contrast (text `#eef4ee` at the given opacity over sampled card pixels)

| Background sampled | RGB | text 100% | text-dim 62% | text-faint 38% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Darkest card area (5th percentile) | 27, 55, 40 | 11.6 | 5.5 | 3.0 |
| Median card, habib-wh 1440 | 57, 94, 77 | 6.5 | 3.6 | 2.3 |
| Median card, habib-wh 430 | 68, 104, 78 | 5.6 | 3.3 | 2.2 |
| Median card, habib-mkh 430 | 64, 107, 88 | 5.4 | 3.2 | 2.1 |
| Dock bars, habib-mkh 430 (10 px dim labels) | 40, 81, 63 | 8.0 | 4.2 | 2.6 |

| Lamp-on card, median (warm glow) | 107, 112, 80 | 4.6 | 2.8 | 2.0 |

Reading:
1. **text-dim ranges from 2.7 to 5.5 and is about 3.2 to 3.6 on a typical card.** It fails 4.5:1 on every card except the darkest ones. v0.1 estimated "about 4:1 on the lightest glass"; the glass is lighter than assumed (the blur lifts the wallpaper and the hero carries a day-part tint).
2. **text-faint ranges from 1.9 to 3.0.** v0.1 estimated 2.5.
3. **Primary text on the hero is 4.4:1.** The 17 px date (weight 500) and the 12 px Hijri line are small text and fall short; the clock and temperature are large text and pass at 3:1.
4. Raising dim to 72% (the v0.1 proposal) gives 3.8 on a median card and 3.1 on the hero: not enough. 4.5:1 on a median card needs about 84% opacity. On the hero no opacity reaches 4.5; the surface there has to be darker.

## Part E. Light and dark

1. Emulating `prefers-color-scheme: light` was not possible in this session, so the visual test was not run.
2. What was read: HA reports `darkMode: true`; the browser reports dark; the profile theme is "Material You"; "Habib Glass" has no `modes` key; no habib card stylesheet contains a `prefers-color-scheme` rule (the only one on the page is HA's own for the page background).
3. Conclusion from the source and the computed styles: every colour in the cards is a literal inside the shadow root, so a light preference cannot change them. Expected result "no change" stands, but it is inferred, not observed.
4. Still unverified: whether a theme set per view follows the profile's light or dark choice (guide section 8).

## Changes the guide needs (most important first)

Proposed edits only; this audit did not edit the guide or the tokens file.

1. **Section 10 gap 2 and tokens:** replace the contrast estimates with the measured table. State that `text-dim` fails AA on typical cards (3.2 to 3.6) and that 72% does not fix it; the fix needs about 84% dim and a darker hero surface. Owner decision.
2. **Section 6.2 rule 2 and `layout.phone-query`:** record the 699 to 700 px gap as a defect; proposed queries `(max-width: 699.98px)` and `(min-width: 700px)`.
3. **Section 6.1 and `layout.columns_*`:** replace the per-device guesses with the column rule and both tables (sidebar docked and hidden). Correct "1440 = 4 columns" to "4 with the sidebar hidden, 3 with it docked".
4. **Section 3.5 item 5 and `size.touch`:** the fold bar is a 60 px target, not 30. Add the three unlisted small controls: media tabs 28.5, media volume 6, avatars 39.8. Room header is 22 high.
5. **Section 3.2 and `type`:** fold-bar value is 17 px (15 in the dock) and fold-bar text is 13 px; degree signs are full colour; add slider value, room counter and dial range; Hijri tracking 0.02em.
6. **Section 5 or 10:** dock bar summaries must fit one line at 193 px; today Climate and Listen wrap. Prayer bar wording after Isha.
7. **Section 3.3:** slider value uses a white literal; either add a token or move it to `text`.
8. **Section 1:** the profile theme is Material You; Habib Glass is per view. List the three leftover resources.
9. **Section 2:** add the article's gaps as rules or as open items: hover state, failed-action feedback, orientation, localisation, performance budget.
10. **Section 6.2:** note the empty space under the hero on wide habib-mkh and the vw-based clock.
